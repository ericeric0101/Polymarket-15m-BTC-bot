  replaces the base quantity (`run_bot.py:3585`). VERIFIED.

## 10. Sellability / partial-fill safety

- **Fees:** maker fills are fee-free (token balance = filled qty). A taker BUY pays its fee in **shares**
  on Protocol V1 and in **USDC** on V2 (`fill_ledger.py:80-104`). A taker SELL pays its fee in USDC.
  Entries are post-only maker (INFERRED: maker in normal operation).
- **Rounding:** size rounds down to 0.01 at the SDK; the ROUND_FLOOR exit quantity is at precision 6 (`taker_exit.py:1020`).
- **Sellable haircut:** sellable = min(confirmed, local, on-chain × 0.999, venue cap); within 10 s of a BUY,
  minus 0.05 sh (`pricing_runtime.py:585-631`). A position is exitable only if fill ≥ 5.005
  (≥ 5.05 inside the after-buy window).
- **Partial fill (e.g. 3 of 10):** counts as the market's one BUY. Nothing tops it up. Every SELL path
  skips quantities < 5. The position is held to settlement and redeemed. No TP, stop or hard breaker
  can act on it, and its loss is bounded by its own notional. INFERRED.
- **Clips below minimum:** the L2/risk clip < 5 → entry **skipped** (`run_bot.py:3596-3602`;
  `order_submission.py:179-211`, tests). A clip into [5.000, 5.005) is **accepted but not sellable** (P2-G).
- **Full actual quantity:** exits sell `min(qty, sellable_qty)`. The TP fraction is 1.0. A partially
  filled TP or exit can leave a remainder < 5 that is unsellable until settlement.
- **TARGET_5_5_SELLABILITY_SAFE = CONDITIONAL.** A **full** 5.5-share maker fill is sellable
  (5.5 × 0.999 = 5.4945 ≥ 5; with a V1 taker-buy fee at p = 0.75, ≈ 5.43). Conditions: (1) the fill is
  complete; (2) the fill is ≥ 5.005 sh (any partial fill below that is hold-to-settlement only);
  (3) no partial exit leaves < 5. 5.5 is sufficient for full fills; it is not raised.
