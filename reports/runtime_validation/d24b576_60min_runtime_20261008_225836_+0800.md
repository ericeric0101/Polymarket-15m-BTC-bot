## 證據限制

- Per-event triggered counts are lower bounds from persisted trigger rows; total independent trigger/suppression counters are not exported. Summary crossing counters can additionally corroborate completed markets.
- Invalidation journals record confirmed/cleared transitions, not every logical cycle increment; exact per-cycle reconstruction may be unavailable.
- Writer queue maximum is the maximum sampled/exported depth, not an instrumented all-time high-water mark.
- Opening gate failure means the requested runtime criterion was not established; it does not alone prove a software root cause. Observed strike readiness after handoff does not prove upstream strike unavailability before the request.
- Opening-delay quantiles exclude the startup-partial market. Readiness classification uses exported timestamps; source/strike/quote distinctions may be inconclusive.
- Freshness-v2 restriction applies to prediction snapshots; unrelated research event types do not carry the same clock-version field.
- 60-minute growth rates are short-window observations, not guaranteed long-term rates.
